FS-UAE (the Amiga emulator)
FS-UAE by Frode Solheim and contributors is free software under the GNU
General Public License, version 2. It is a separate program, not part of
ADAM: ADAM only starts it to play the disks you build.
On Windows, an unchanged copy of FS-UAE 3.2.35 comes with ADAM, in the FS-UAE
folder next to ADAM.exe; its licences are in FS-UAE/Licenses. Its source code
(fs-uae-3.2.35.tar.xz) is on ADAM's itch.io page next to the ADAM downloads,
and at https://github.com/FrodeSolheim/fs-uae/releases/tag/v3.2.35
On a Mac, ADAM downloads the unchanged official FS-UAE 3.2.35 release for your
Mac (Apple silicon or Intel) from that page into its own folder the first time
you press Build & play; the same page has its source code.
On other systems, get FS-UAE from https://fs-uae.net
