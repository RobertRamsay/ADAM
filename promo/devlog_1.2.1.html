ADAM 1.2.1 is a small update with one big aim: every feature you can use, you can now learn inside ADAM, in ADAM Lite as well as Pro. There are a few fixes too.

Tutorials for everything

Press F1 on any page (or use Learn ADAM) and its tutorial now covers everything the page can do. Until now some features were only taught in the Pro lessons, even though ADAM Lite has them. These now have their own steps:

  • Actors: BOBs, shots, flyers, options and effects, enemies that fire by themselves, copies, and pick up and throw.
  • Tiles and Levels: tools (pickaxe, hammer, ladders, sword), stamps and spawners for shoot 'em up maps, animated tiles (Pro), and scrolling.
  • Logic: items and recipes, AND DO lines, IF lines, and groups inside groups.
  • Backdrop, Sprites and Animate: named palettes, onion skin, bigger sprites, and Hit and Die animations.
  • Sound and Music: the Patch synth, filter sweeps, sample loop points, note effects and saving a .mod.
  • Home and Build: interface size and the second disk.

Each step now opens the right tab for you, so the highlight always lands on the thing it's talking about. Some older steps were out of date (one pointed at a switch that no longer exists) and have been rewritten.

Fixed

  • Deleting a song now renumbers your Play music rules, so they keep playing the same songs.
  • Removing a level now moves the later levels' music, palette and Joins on level actors up with them.
  • Undo after deleting a palette now also puts back the Switch palette rules that used it.
  • Using fewer colours, or switching an A1200 project to the A500 / A600, now moves tile pictures and hill colours to the nearest colour left, as it already did for the backdrop. Before, they could come out in the wrong colours on the Amiga.

Getting it

Download the installer (_Setup.exe) or the zip from this page. If you already have ADAM 1.0.1 or later, it will tell you the update is out when you start it. FS-UAE still comes with ADAM, and your projects open as they are.
