ADAM 1.3.3 is out, for Windows and macOS. This one is about how things look and how smoothly they run: a new look for the editor, an option that keeps busy games moving at an even pace, redrawn example games, and shoot 'em up waves that snake across the screen. As always, everything is in ADAM Lite as well as Pro.

A steady 25 frames a second

A few of you told me the side-scrolling shoot 'em up slowed down when a lot of shots were flying. On an Amiga 500 / 600 a busy screen can't always keep up 50 frames a second, so the game sped up and slowed down as the screen filled and emptied. The Build page now has A steady 25 frames a second: every frame takes two screen frames, so the game moves at one even pace however busy it gets. Everything moves half as far in a second, so set speeds and timers to suit. Games that keep up 50 can leave it off.

Starfire's and Nebula Strike's A500 / 600 versions now have it switched on, and Starfire's is lighter (3 saucers and 2 darts at a time). On the A1200, Nebula Strike uses 64 colours and Starfire 32, which leaves the blitter more time.

Shoot 'em ups: new art and snaking waves

The shoot 'em up examples have new, bigger sprites, shaded like metal and glass: ships, saucers, the gunship and the boss, with explosions as puffs of fire. In Nebula Strike a row of darts now follows one track and snakes across the screen instead of bobbing up and down together, and the enemies come one wave at a time: six darts, then spinners, a heavy and a seeker. That keeps about six enemies on screen at once. Open it in the editor to see how the waves are set up: they're timed rules.

Redrawn example games

The single-screen examples have arcade-style redraws: every sprite is outlined in its darkest colour and lit from the upper left, so it reads clearly against the backdrop.

  • Deep Dive: a brass-helmeted diver with a porthole and flippers, a shark with gills and a forked tail, domed jellyfish and shiny pearls.
  • Firefly Night: an owl with ear tufts and talons, bats that show up against the night sky, and glowing fireflies.
  • Dig Deep: the Miner turned side-on, with every walk, jump, pickaxe and sword frame redrawn, plus a new bat and mole.
  • Torchlight: a side-on adventurer with a four-step walk.
  • Gem Hunter and Piston Pass: the ball hero has little hands that swing as he walks.

A new look

The editor has a new skin: gunmetal panels with a silver rim, satin-metal buttons that sink when you press them, and a brushed-metal background. Everything is where it was; it just looks more like a machine.

Submit your game

Made something in ADAM? Home > Community > Submit your game sends me its title, who made it and a link to the project, and I'll add it to the Community list. Games in the list are shared as their projects, so everything in yours will be open for others to look at and learn from. ADAM asks you to confirm that before it sends anything.

Crash reports

If ADAM ever crashes, the next time you start it, it asks whether to send me a short report: the error, the version, the page you were on, and how many actors and rules the project has. Nothing else is in it, and you can add a note. Nothing is sent unless you press Send, and you can switch the question off.

On a Mac

The Build page has an Emulator choice: FS-UAE as before, or AMULET or vAmiga if you have one of them installed.

Getting it

Download the Windows installer or zip, or the macOS .dmg or app zip, from this page. Your projects open as they are, and FS-UAE still comes with ADAM on Windows. The source code of that FS-UAE is now on this page too, as the GPL asks.

If a game of yours still struggles to keep up on an A500, try the steady 25 option and tell me in the comments how it goes.
