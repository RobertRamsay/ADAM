ADAM - the Amiga Dev and Art Machine
Make Amiga games. No code needed.

ADAM is a no-code tool that makes real, bootable Amiga floppy disks (.ADF) for the Amiga 500 / 600 and the Amiga 1200. Paint a backdrop, draw your sprites, place your actors, write a few rules in plain sentences, and press Build. The game boots in the emulator straight away, and the same disk runs on a Gotek or a real floppy on real hardware.

How it works

  • Paint the backdrop - a 320 x 256 picture, up to 16 colours on the A500 / 600 and up to 128 on the A1200, with a copper sky behind it. Import a PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP or Amiga IFF picture; one with a palette that fits keeps its own colours exactly. Named palettes can be switched or faded in by a rule.
  • Draw sprites - hardware sprites, and bigger BOBs drawn by the blitter. Import a picture or a whole sprite sheet and ADAM cuts it into frames.
  • Animate - Idle, Walk, Jump and Fall, eight directions, animations while fire is held, and Hit and Die. Drag animations between actors on the Animate page.
  • Place actors - players, platform heroes, bouncers, patrols (straight, diagonal or easing in and out), chasers, hoppers, fallers, shots, flyers, options and effects. Any actor can be a moving platform, or a column that reaches the floor.
  • Write the rules - "WHEN the hero touches the key, DO add 100 to the score AND DO open the door". IF lines check items, where an actor is, or a counter. Items and recipes, health, time limits, lives, goals, game over and win screens.
  • Build levels - up to 10 levels, with rolling hills, or a scrolling tile world you can dig and build in, with a map for every level.
  • Shoot 'em ups - scrolling in any direction, or climbing a tile map, with enemy waves, power-ups and bosses.
  • Sound and music - a chip-sound synth, a three-oscillator patch synth and WAV import for sounds; a Music page with loops and a step editor, ProTracker .mod import and export.
  • A HUD of your own - a framed panel with your own font, score, lives, items, health and time.
  • Build and play - one click (or F5) builds the .ADF and boots it in FS-UAE, which comes with ADAM. God mode and Test a level help you try things out.

Learn as you go

The Learn ADAM button opens a tutorial for the page you are on, a guided tour, and step-by-step lessons from "Walkabout" (5 minutes) up to a full game. The example games open in the editor, so you can see how each one is made.

ADAM Lite and ADAM Pro

ADAM Lite is free and has every feature - it makes complete games, nothing is held back. ADAM Pro raises the ceilings and adds more to learn from:

  • Up to 32 actors on the disk (Lite: 12), sprites and BOBs up to 64 x 64 (Lite: 32 x 32), and column pictures up to 256 high.
  • 16 frames an animation (Lite: 6), 128 sprite slots (Lite: 64), 64 sounds (Lite: 8).
  • Tile worlds with 512 tile types and maps up to 1024 x 1024 (Lite: 96 and 256 x 256).
  • A loading screen of your own, animated tiles, drawing your own HUD font, and snap to the grid on the Music page.
  • Six extra example games - Firefly Night, Dig Deep, Nebula Strike, Starfire, Jade Serpent and Torchlight - and the Pro lessons: BOBs, a shoot 'em up, a loading screen and items.

Lite comes with Gem Hunter, Deep Dive, Star Catcher, Dodge Ball and Piston Pass. Every example also comes in an Amiga 1200 version.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS.
  • That's it. On Windows, FS-UAE comes with ADAM. On a Mac, ADAM downloads the official FS-UAE for your Mac the first time you press Build and play. You can also point ADAM at an FS-UAE you already have.
  • Optional: your own Kickstart ROM. Without one, the AROS Kickstart that comes with FS-UAE is used.
