ADAM 1.3.0 is out. The biggest change is on the Logic page: rules can now do a lot more, much of it borrowed from my C64 no-code tool. There are moving platforms, a map for every level, better ways to test, and nicer example games too. As always, everything is in ADAM Lite as well as Pro.

Smarter rules

New things a rule can wait for (WHEN):

  • Actor reaches an edge - left, right, top or bottom. A ball that gets past the bat, an enemy walking off the side.
  • Actor hits the scenery - a solid tile in a tile world, or the hills and painted ground.
  • The counter reaches a number - five switches pulled opens the door, every tenth coin gives a life.
  • The time runs out - see time limits below.

New things a rule can do (DO):

  • Add one to the counter.
  • Lose health and Add health.
  • Bounce actor back - left-right, up-down or both. Patrols and bouncers turn round, shots fly back, anything else steps back to where it was.
  • Stick actor to another and Let go of actor - a frog on a log, a ball held on a bat until fire is pressed.
  • Stop all sounds, Pause music and Resume music.

And IF lines can now ask where an actor is: above, below, level with, left of or right of another one, with a margin in pixels. So "WHEN the hero touches a slime, IF the hero is above it, DO squash it" - and a second rule for when it isn't.

Health and time limits

On the Home page you can give each life some health: Lose health takes a bit at a time, and only at 0 does a life go. On the Levels page each level can have a time limit that counts down. Both show on the score row, or as Health, Time left and Counter fields on a custom HUD (UI page).

Moving platforms and patrols

Any actor can now be stood on, so a patrol becomes a lift or a ferry the player rides. Patrols can also be given a distance, and the Actors page shows the tops of platforms and how far each patrol goes.

A map for every level

In a tile world each level can now have a map of its own (Tiles page, Map for). If a game gets too big for one disk, the level maps can go on a second disk, as level music already could.

Testing

  • God mode (Build page): your lives never run out while testing, and F8 skips to the next level.
  • Each level has a Test button that starts the game straight on that level.

Neither changes the disk you make for other people.

Example games

The sprites in the examples are rounder and shaded now, with better animations. Gem Hunter has a whole new cast, and the miner in Dig Deep bends into each dig.

Fixed and improved

  • The Logic page now allows 128 rules - the same as the Amiga engine - instead of stopping at 100.
  • Clicking where two actors overlap on the Actors page picks the one in front.
  • Sound previews play in both speakers instead of only the left.
  • If a floppy can't be read, the game tries again before giving up, and shows a plain yellow screen instead of starting broken.
  • Move actor to a random spot in a tile world now picks a clear spot on screen, not somewhere inside the rock.

Getting it

Download the Windows installer (_Setup.exe) or zip, or the macOS .dmg or .app.zip, from this page. If you already have ADAM 1.0.1 or later, it will tell you the update is out when you start it. FS-UAE still comes with ADAM, and your projects open as they are.
