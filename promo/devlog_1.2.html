ADAM 1.2 is a smaller update than 1.1, but you'll see it as soon as ADAM opens. Here's what's changed since 1.1.0.

Added

A new Home page. How it works and the Example games now sit side by side, and every example game shows a picture of its first screen (the A1200 version's picture when you've picked the A1200). Click a game to load it, or double-click it to build a disk and play it in FS-UAE straight away.

Animated tiles (Pro). Tiles in a tile world can now animate: water, lava, torches, flickering lights. On the Tiles page, give a tile 2 to 8 pictures in its new Animation box, pick a speed and Loop or Ping-pong, and watch the preview. The tile still behaves as itself (solid, dig, ladder and so on); only its picture changes. The water and torches in Dig Deep show how it looks.

Improved

  • Faster BOBs. The blitter now draws only the part of each BOB that has pixels in it. Busy games with lots of BOBs drop far fewer frames, and Starfire on the A1200 now keeps up almost all the time.
  • Flash the screen is now Flash the player. A flashing screen wasn't pleasant to play, so this DO now makes the player blink instead, the same blink a hit gives. Your rules keep working as they are.
  • Small screens. On a smaller laptop (1366 x 768, or 1080p with Windows set to 150%), ADAM's window could run off the bottom of the screen. Now ADAM shrinks to fit, and every page keeps the same layout.

Fixed

  • The Sound page's Play sound button could end up half off the panel on a 1280 x 720 screen. It now stays in view.
  • On the Tiles page, the first-screen box and the actors no longer draw past the edge of the map.
  • The tip under the Music page's loop list no longer runs out of its panel.
  • Lowering the bitplanes, or switching an A1200 project to the A500 / A600, no longer leaves a custom HUD in colours that are gone.

Known issue

  • Starfire still drops frames on the A500 / A600 when the screen is very busy. The A1200 version is fine.

Getting it

Download the installer (_Setup.exe) or the zip from this page. FS-UAE still comes with ADAM, and projects from earlier versions open as they are.

Thanks to everyone who has tried ADAM and sent feedback. If something doesn't work the way you expect, please tell me in the comments.
