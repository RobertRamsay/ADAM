ADAM 1.3.2 is out. This one is about bringing your own art in: the Sprites page can now import pictures and whole sprite sheets, and pictures with a palette (Amiga IFF, 8-bit PNG, GIF, BMP) keep their own colours. There are also columns, easing patrols, a few new rules and a new free example game, Piston Pass. As always, everything is in ADAM Lite as well as Pro.

Import pictures and sprite sheets

On the Sprites page, Import picture or sheet... loads a PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP or Amiga IFF picture or brush (Deluxe Paint, Personal Paint and so on). One picture becomes one sprite. A sheet - a grid of frames - fills that slot and the ones after it. You set:

  • the cell size, the margin and the spacing between cells, with the cells outlined on the picture as you go;
  • how many to take, and whether to leave out empty cells;
  • what is see-through: the picture's own transparency (an IFF brush's mask or transparent colour, a PNG's alpha), or also the colour in the top-left corner, for sheets on a plain magenta or black background;
  • the colours: one set of 3 for the whole sheet (the frames of one animation), each sprite its own 3, or - for a picture with a palette - the picture's own colour numbers.

That last one is for Amiga artists: draw a sprite in colours 17, 18 and 19, as on the real machine, and they land on pens 1, 2 and 3 with your palette's colours. Each sprite is made the smallest size that holds a cell.

Backdrops keep their palette

The Backdrop page's Import picture... now takes IFF, BMP and 8-bit PNG and GIF pictures too. A picture with a palette that fits the backdrop's colours comes in exactly as it was drawn - same pixels, same palette, no dithering. An overscan picture is cut to 320 x 256 round its middle. Anything else is fitted and its colours picked as before.

Columns

On the Actors page an actor can Reach the floor: a pillar, a piston or a tree trunk drawn right down to the bottom of the screen, and its sides are walls the player can't walk through. A column that has stopped costs nothing to draw, so a screen full of them stays smooth. In ADAM Pro a sprite can be 128 or 256 high, for a column's whole picture.

Patrols

  • Eases in and out: a patrol slows down near each end and is fastest in the middle, like a piston on a crank or a swing - much easier to time a jump on.
  • Diagonal: a patrol can go at 45 degrees, for sloping lifts and enemies that sweep across.

New rules

  • Stop actor where it is and Start actor moving again.
  • Start the level again - the level card, everyone back home, the counter back to 0; score, lives and items stay.
  • A new WHEN: Fire pressed, stick held up, down, left or right - down + fire to try a level again, up + fire to use a door.
  • IF lines can check the counter: is, at least, or below a number. With it, one card after another can happen in turn - the first press does one thing, the next press the next.
  • Holding fire now sets off one rule card per press, so a sequence of fire cards can't run away with you.

New example: Piston Pass

A free example and a template for "set it up, then cross" games. Seven pistons rise and sink in a factory pit, one at a time; press fire to stop each one at the right height. When all seven are set, the worker is yours: walk and jump across them to the exit - a piston's side is a wall, so the steps have to be small enough to climb. Fall into the pit or meet the bat and you lose a life and set the floor again; if you've left yourself stuck, hold fire for a moment to give up and try the floor again (that costs a life too). Eight floors, the pistons faster each time. Open it in the editor to see how it works - it uses columns, easing, the counter and the new rules.

Getting it

Download the Windows installer (_Setup.exe) or zip, or the macOS .dmg or .app.zip, from this page. If you already have ADAM 1.0.1 or later, it will tell you the update is out when you start it. FS-UAE still comes with ADAM, and your projects open as they are.

If you import your own art and something doesn't come out the way you drew it, please tell me in the comments - and send the picture if you can.
